## 📌 Project Overview

The Event Management System is a full-stack web application that allows users to manage events, view schedules, add notes, and track updates through a user-friendly interface. This project emphasizes simplicity, making it easier for individuals and teams to coordinate their events effectively.

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication**: Create an account and log in to keep your information secure.
- 📅 **Event Creation and Management**: Organize your events with ease.
- 📝 **Notes and Task Tracking**: Keep track of updates and important tasks related to your events.
- 📊 **Overview Dashboard**: Get a quick look at your upcoming events.
- 🔎 **Event Filtering and Search**: Quickly find the events you need.
- 🎨 **Responsive UI Design**: Enjoy a seamless experience across all devices.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

### Frontend

## 🙋‍♂️ User Guide

### Creating an Account
- Open the application.
- Click on the "Sign Up" button.
- Fill out the required information and submit the form.

### Logging In
- Open the application.
- Click on the "Login" button.
- Enter your credentials and click "Sign In."

### Managing Events
- Once logged in, navigate to the dashboard.
- Click "Create Event" to enter your event details.
- Save your changes to see your event on the dashboard.

### Adding Notes
- Select an event from the dashboard.
- Click on the “Add Note” button.
- Type your note and save it.

### Searching for Events
- Use the search bar located at the top of the dashboard.
- Type keywords related to your event and hit enter.
